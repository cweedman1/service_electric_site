The original artwork

More than a mascot.

Ryan’s father created the original hand-drawn lightbulb character. Ryan later added the angel wings and halo after his father passed away.

The site preserves that drawing as source artwork. Its warmth shapes the visual identity, but the character is not redrawn, modernized, or used as decoration everywhere.
