A family electrical business since 1987.

From father to son

The name stayed in the family.

Ryan’s father started Service Electric in 1987. The work was local, practical, and built around serving customers across the Dallas-Fort Worth area.

Ryan later continued the business. Today, he is the owner and the person customers can call directly.

The company still carries the same name, the same original artwork, and a straightforward approach to electrical work.
